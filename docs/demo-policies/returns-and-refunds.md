# Returns and refunds

You can return any item within 45 days of delivery, unused and in its original packaging. Bikes and e-bikes are the exception: they can be returned within 14 days and must have been ridden less than 25 kilometres.

To start a return, open a ticket with the order number. We send a prepaid PostNL return label within one working day. Returns from outside the Netherlands are not prepaid; the customer pays the shipping cost.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days after we receive the return. Shipping costs of the original order are refunded only when the whole order is returned.

A late delivery entitles the customer to a goodwill refund of 10% of the order total, at most EUR 25, when the parcel arrives more than 5 working days after the promised date. Support agents approve these refunds; they are never automatic.

Damaged items: report damage within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We replace the item or refund it in full, including shipping, and the customer does not have to send it back.
