# Delivery and warranty

Orders placed before 16:00 on a working day ship the same day. We ship with PostNL for parcels under 10 kg, with DHL for parcels from 10 kg, and with UPS for deliveries outside the Benelux.

Customers can change the delivery address once, free of charge, until the parcel is out for delivery. After that the carrier charges EUR 4.95 for a redirect.

Warranty on bikes is 5 years on the frame and 2 years on all other parts. Helmets and clothing have a 2 year warranty. Batteries of e-bikes have a warranty of 2 years or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

A warranty claim needs the order number and a description or photo of the defect. The warranty does not cover wear parts such as tyres, brake pads and chains.

Password reset emails are sent within 5 minutes. If none arrives, check the spam folder; after 30 minutes a support agent can reset the password manually.
